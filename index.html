The pattern, made buildable — not a pitch

If cooperation is co-regulation,
you can build the room for it.

One vacant Mid-Market storefront → the smallest viable unit of civic nervous-system regulation. A sauna, a sound bath, a bodywork space. Local artists and healers as the anchor team. Free to sliding scale.
Not $80 wellness. Not crisis intervention at the edge. The developmental middle the corridor never had — the held space where a borrowed nervous system gets lent until your own comes back online.
I call it ROOM. It's one instance. The point is the pattern.
